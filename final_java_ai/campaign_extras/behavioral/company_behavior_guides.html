Company Behavior Guides (beyond Amazon/Google)

Full question bank + prep grid: 39_behavioral_question_bank.md · 40_behavioral_prep_grid.md (adapted from awesome-behavioral-interviews; answers are Tarun-specific).

Use with star_bank.md. Pick 2–3 stories per company. Never reuse the same story twice in one loop.

Amazon

Focus LPs: Ownership Dive Deep Deliver Results Hire and Develop Bias for Action Earn Trust Have Backbone. Stories: FRM ownership (7-ish) Masters migration (8) idempotency near-miss (10) CH disagreement (1).

Google

Googleyness: humility ambiguity collaboration bias to action. Stories: CH verdict (1) malformed-plan validator (3) copilot inversion (4).

Microsoft

Growth mindset customer obsession inclusive collaboration. Stories: anti-bot arms race (9) coverage-gap fix (6) mentorship Masters.

LinkedIn

Transformation craft collaboration. Stories: Kafka scale Masters Menu $600K craft quality RAG eval.

Apple

Quality privacy detail ownership. Stories: Menu 98%/100% schema FRM audit correctness column-aliasing bug.

Netflix

Freedom and responsibility context not control candor. Stories: on-call Masters (EM round) owning mistake constants-refactor (5) high agency Menu anti-bot.

Atlassian

Play as a team open company build with heart. Stories: led 3 design reviews ORM-vs-repository disagreement (7) mentorship.

Salesforce

Trust customer success. Stories: FRM PwC trust path IRP idempotency (10) compliance ANZ 99.9%.
