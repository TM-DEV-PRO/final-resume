Study Handbook — start here (Java + Python + Go track)

What this is. A plain-English handbook for explaining every project, technology and number on the resume Tarun_Mittal_SSE_5yr_Java_PyGo_AI.pdf, in depth and in your own words. Every chapter defines its terms, shows diagrams and worked examples, explains why each design was chosen, and ends with practice questions and a whiteboard script.

This is the Java + Python + Go track. Stack wording here: Uber FRM is Spring Boot + Spring Data JPA / Hibernate. Masters India is Spring Boot. Impact Analytics is Go/Gin for the platform and Python for the AI agents.

Study plans

If you have one evening. Read 01 (intros), 02 (numbers), and the one-minute version plus the "Say this / don't say this" box of chapters 03–07.

If you have three days. - Day 1: 01, 02, 03, 04. Practise the IA whiteboard scripts out loud. - Day 2: 05, 06, 07, 08. Practise each one-minute version. - Day 3: 11 (pick 6 stories and rehearse), skim 09 and 10 for anything unfamiliar, then do the practice drills at the end of each project chapter without looking.

If you have a week. Add one deep-dive doc per day from prep/ (listed below) and do every practice drill twice, once out loud.

How to answer any question (the shape that works)

  1. Direct answer first, in one sentence.
  2. Context: what the system was and why it mattered.
  3. What I did: the design, with one concrete detail.
  4. Result: one or two numbers, said the way chapter 2 shows.
  5. What I learned or would change.

Keep it to about 60–90 seconds, then stop and let them pick the next thread.

The honesty rules (they protect you)

• Uber work was via EPAM. ANZ is historical Uber Mobility work. Menu 98% is offline evaluation.
• 80% is a CI promotion gate, not live accuracy for all tenants. 73% and 74% come from a separate 200-case benchmark.
• 15.5× is query time. The 170 GB OOM is load time. They're not cause and effect.
• 344k is rows per second, not requests per second. The 4.09B copy was in flight.
• Partition rollback means drop the partition and recopy, not an atomic swap.
• At Impact Analytics there's no Kafka, Flink, CDC, Kubernetes operations, Milvus or human review queue. Cluster Copilot and Hindsight are verbal-only depth. LangSmith is Ask Iris only.
• "Led 3" is Uber FRM. "Mentored 2" is Masters India. Don't merge them.
• Kubernetes, Terraform, Spark: study-only / literacy. Never "I operated clusters".
• Never share cluster hosts, passwords or .env values. Don't offer to open-source the copier.
