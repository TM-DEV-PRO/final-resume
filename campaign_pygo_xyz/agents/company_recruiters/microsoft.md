# Microsoft Technical Recruiter Screen

## Typical titles and levels

| Posting title | Typical level | YoE on public postings (2025–2026) | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software Engineer II | 61 / 62 band (commonly “SWE II”) | Often **2+** (some **2–5**) | Solid match |
| Senior Software Engineer | 63 / 64 | Often **4+** to **8+** depending on org | **Primary senior target** if ownership is clear |
| Principal Software Engineer | 65+ | Staff-like scope | Not yet |

**Practical targeting:** Prefer **Senior Software Engineer** postings where quals say ~4+ years and Python is listed. Use **Software Engineer II** as fallback for stricter teams or thinner ownership packaging. Azure org / Microsoft AI / Copilot-adjacent teams may weight agentic AI higher.

**Title map:** Your “Senior SWE” (IA) + SDE2 (Uber/Masters) → SWE II solid; Senior if FRM + leadership + IA AI story are visible in top third of resume.

## Hard requirements recruiters check

### Years of experience
- SWE II: frequently **Bachelor’s + 2+** years (or equivalent).
- Senior: frequently **Bachelor’s + 4+** (sometimes 6–8+ preferred) with coding in C#, Java, C/C++, JavaScript, or **Python**.
- “OR equivalent experience” is common — degree is not always a hard kill.

### Languages
- **Python** is explicitly accepted on many modern postings (including AI/Copilot-adjacent).
- **C#** still dominates some classic Microsoft service teams — lack of C# is **not** an automatic reject for Python-listed roles, but ask the recruiter.
- **Go** is a plus for cloud-native/infra-ish teams; less universal than Python/C#.

### Systems
- Scalable services, distributed systems, cloud (Azure preferred on many teams; AWS/GCP experience often transferable).
- AI-native postings: LLMs, agentic workflows, Copilot-style integration — your IA work is relevant **when honest about building vs fully shipped**.

### Leadership
- Senior: mentoring, technical leadership, guiding project plans — your led 3 / mentored 2 helps.
- SWE II: collaboration + ownership sufficient.

## Pass vs borderline vs fail (this candidate)

### Pass
- Senior/SWE II role listing **Python** + services/AI: your stack matches.
- Clear ownership (FRM design) + leadership headcounts.
- IA agentic story framed as **engineering systems around LLMs**, not prompt dabbling.
- Recruiter phone: collaborative tone, learning agility, concrete customer impact.

### Borderline
- Classic Azure/.NET platform role with **C# hard requirement** — screen may stall unless recruiter accepts Python+Go transfer.
- “Senior” posting with **8+ preferred** — 5y may be downleveled to SWE II (acceptable outcome).
- Consulting/EPAM framing without Uber product ownership → weaker “Microsoft senior” read.

### Fail
- Claiming shipped Copilot-scale AI products or fake Azure certifications.
- Pure tool laundry list with no ownership.
- Behavioral red flags: blame culture, inability to discuss trade-offs, arrogance about level.

## Highest-impact honest resume flips (no fake years)

1. **Mirror posting language:** If role says “agentic AI workflows,” put IA Copilot/tool-gate architecture near the top.
2. **Senior signal block:** “Led design reviews for 3 engineers; mentored 2; owned FRM architecture.”
3. **Python first, Go second** for Microsoft ATS; keep Go visible for cloud-native teams.
4. **Cloud transferable phrasing:** object storage, managed data stores, observability — without inventing Azure depth.
5. **One reliability story** (Masters fault tolerance / p95) for “production-ready” keywords.
6. **ClickHouse as analytical systems skill** for data/AI platform teams.
7. **Testing discipline** (FRM pytest volume) — Microsoft postings often mention quality/SDLC.
8. **No fake C#** — offer “production Python/Go; willing to ramp on C#/Azure stack” only in conversation if asked.

## Culture / behavior screen notes (growth mindset & Microsoft values)

Microsoft interview culture emphasizes:
- **Growth mindset** — learn new stacks, seek feedback, iterate
- **Customer obsession** — who benefited and how
- **Collaborate / one Microsoft** — cross-team, inclusive collaboration
- **Deliver success through clarity & accountability**
- **Diversity & inclusion** — respect, listening (light touch, authentic)

**Story mapping:**
| Theme | Honest hooks |
|---|---|
| Growth mindset | PHP→Python FastAPI; adding Go; agentic + CH learning curve |
| Customer | Planners (IA); auditors (FRM); enterprise GST clients (Masters) |
| Collaboration | EPAM↔Uber; design reviews; API contracts |
| Accountability | Owned recon migration; CI gates; production on-call habits |
| Ambiguity | Building Copilot against targets (under 1 hour / failure-rate goals) — say **building**, not shipped |

**Phone tip:** Microsoft recruiters often discuss **leveling early**. Accept SWE II if Senior is blocked; negotiate with evidence later. Emphasize teachability on Azure/.NET if the team is classic Microsoft services.
