# Atlassian Technical Recruiter Screen

## Typical titles and levels

| Title | Typical band | YoE on public postings | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software Engineer | Mid | ~2–5y | Possible |
| **Senior Software Engineer** | Senior IC (P40/P50-style community labels vary) | Often **5+** / **6–10** on some Bengaluru/US postings | **Primary target** |
| Principal Engineer | Staff+ | Broad technical leadership | Not default |
| Data / Platform Senior SWE | Senior | Big data + software hybrid | Strong if Kafka/CH emphasized |

Atlassian cares about **Team Anywhere / distributed collaboration**, **cloud product engineering**, and **values-aligned behavior** as much as raw algorithms.

**Practical targeting:** **Senior Software Engineer** on backend, platform, or data platform teams. Data-platform postings that list Kafka/Python/Java/Kotlin are especially aligned.

---

## Hard requirements recruiters check

### Years of experience
- Senior roles commonly **5+** or **6+** years for data/platform tracks.
- ~5y is on the edge for “6–10 years” postings — still apply if skills match; expect senior vs mid calibration.

### Languages
- **Java / Kotlin** very common on product backend.
- **Python**, **Scala** common on data platforms.
- **Go** less universal than Java/Kotlin but acceptable on some platform teams.
- Ask recruiter if JVM is required.

### Systems
- Microservices, cloud (AWS heavily referenced), Kafka/event streaming
- Reliability, scalability, operational excellence
- Data platforms: Spark/Flink/Databricks appear on specialist reqs — only claim what you own
- Building services ground-up: requirements → design → deploy → own

### Leadership
- Mentoring and design ownership expected at senior
- Cross-team collaboration in a distributed company

## Pass vs borderline vs fail (this candidate)

### Pass
- Senior backend/data role listing **Kafka + Python + distributed systems**.
- Clear service ownership (FRM) + streaming reliability (Masters).
- Mentorship present; collaborative tone.
- ClickHouse/analytics angle for data platform roles.

### Borderline
- **Java/Kotlin-hard** product teams — may need explicit ramp willingness or alternate team routing.
- Postings requiring **6–10y** or deep Spark/Flink ownership — your Flink/Spark should stay off resume unless evidenced; do not invent.
- Vendor/client packaging without product impact story.
- AI-agent headline for a Jira platform role (mismatch).

### Fail
- Fake AWS specialty depth or big-data ownership.
- Claiming Atlassian product domain expertise you lack.
- No collaboration/mentorship signal for senior.
- Overstating Kubernetes/Terraform ops.

## Highest-impact honest resume flips (no fake years)

1. **Kafka + delivery semantics language** you can defend: idempotency, retries, DLQ replay (Masters).
2. **Ground-up service ownership:** FRM requirements→design→APIs→tests.
3. **Cloud-honest phrasing:** managed services / object storage experience without fake AWS cert stacks.
4. **Python primary for data/platform; mention Go**; discuss JVM ramp in calls if needed.
5. **Team impact wording:** how your systems helped other engineers/analysts/auditors work better (Atlassian sells teamwork).
6. **Leadership:** led 3 / mentored 2 with design reviews — distributed-team leadership signal.
7. **ClickHouse** for analytics/data platform JDs; pair with measured pivot speedup only.
8. **Keep IA agentic** for AI-tooling teams; otherwise second-tier.

## Culture / behavior screen notes (Atlassian values)

Atlassian publicly emphasizes values along the lines of:
- **Open company, no bullshit** — candor, transparency
- **Build with heart and balance** — sustainable delivery, empathy
- **Don’t #@!% the customer** — customer outcome obsession (teams using Atlassian products)
- **Play, as a team** — collaboration, credit-sharing
- **Be the change you seek** — initiative, continuous improvement

**Interview/behavior practicals:**
- Distributed work: async communication, documentation, design writing
- Inclusive collaboration
- Pragmatism over heroics

**Story mapping:**
| Value | Hooks |
|---|---|
| Open / no bullshit | Honest status on IA building vs shipped; clear ownership boundaries |
| Customer | Auditors, planners, GST enterprise clients as “customers of the system” |
| Team play | Led 3 via contracts/CI; mentored 2; design reviews |
| Be the change | Proposed Sheets→MySQL; PHP→FastAPI strangler; CH for planner scale |
| Heart & balance | Sustainable quality (tests, alerts) vs burnout hero story |

**Phone tip:** Atlassian recruiters often care about **location/timezone (Team Anywhere)** and values fit early. Be ready to explain distributed collaboration habits. If Java is required, say ramp plan — do not put Java on the resume without real experience.
